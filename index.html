Enter your scores

Line scores

Type the ten two-letter codes exactly as they appear on your score report (for example, GT 110). These are “composite” line scores — not your percentile or your raw subtest scores.

Medical profile

Almost everyone entering has a “111111” profile (all 1s means no known medical restrictions). We’ll assume that’s you unless you say otherwise.

If your official profile has any number other than 1, enter it below. “Not yet rated” means you haven’t been through a medical screening yet.
